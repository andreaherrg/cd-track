Most patients mix components

Split the posture into its components, take the rows for each one from the table, then translate every side to the patient. Example: right torticollis, left laterocollis and left shoulder elevation.

  • LEFTSplenius capitis and cervicis · upper trapezius · scalenes

  • LEFTLevator scapulae · trapezius

The left trapezius appears in all three components. Both splenii appear, one for each component: the exam decides which one is active.

Safety limits

Adults: 400 U per 3 months across all indications, ≤ 50 U per injection site, SCM total ≤ 100 U. Ranges in the table are per muscle per session. OnabotulinumtoxinA units do not convert to other botulinum toxins.

Reading the sides

Opposite the chin means contralateral to the rotation; chin side means ipsilateral. In laterocollis every muscle is on the side of the tilt. Antero- and retrocollis use both sides.

How to find it

Palpation landmarks and activation maneuvers are general guidance. Confirm deep muscles (splenius cervicis, longissimus, scalenes) with EMG or ultrasound.
